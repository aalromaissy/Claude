## 2026-06-06 - SwiftUI Icon-Only Button Accessibility\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in SwiftUI (using `Image(systemName: ...)` as the label) do not provide meaningful text to screen readers like VoiceOver by default. This makes primary actions like 'add', 'delete', or 'favorite' inaccessible.\n**Action:** Always append the `.accessibilityLabel("Meaningful description")` modifier to any SwiftUI `Button` where the label consists solely of an icon. For toggleable states (e.g., favorites), use a ternary operator or state variable to provide dynamic, state-aware labels.
